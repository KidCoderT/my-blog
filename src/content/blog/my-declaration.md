This world is so amazing.

I mean seriously look at all the people in the world. The poetry, the artistry, the mastery.

The scientific breakthrough, the mathematical ingenuity, the philosophical debacles.

There is so much abundance in this world that it makes you want to chase everything. But at the same time there is so much distraction so much suffering and so many misunderstandings that can make a persons heart grow cold and lose their wonder.Child like glee exists in each of us, so does deep pain. What matters is what we choose and the decisions we make and how grateful we are for the gifts we are given.

It's crazy to think how my life has changed and also not changed. Crazy to realise how foolish i have been but also how blessed. And also crazy to realise how many amazing people I've met in my life and keep meeting. Why am I writing this idk. It's 3am motivation I guess. But it's nice to stare out the window and dream of a future unafraid of what AI can or cannot do and imagine the possibility of what can be done in the future. I want to dream that's my decleration and i want to live proud 😁. Hope you guys living great lives and if your not we can share a chat.

> life's abundant but only if we are willing to see the sapling in the crust growing after the forest fire burnt it all away
