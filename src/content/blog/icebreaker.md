## Version 2

Mr./Madam Toastmaster, fellow members, and guests.

"Icebreaker, Icebreaker, Icebreaker..." *Pace around the stage + each word getting more tense*

*Stand straight facing the audience*

Honestly, this ice has been breaking my head for about three weeks now. 

I kept spiraling, wondering, and dislocating abstract concepts in my brain only to realise its freaking empty.

So I took a break, went back to the Toastmasters project manual. *Act as though holding a paper with a list* 

It said:
1. The purpose is to introduce yourself. *Turn to the Audidence* Hey guys my name is Tejas! *Turn back to the paper*
2. The audience should know you better by the end.

Hmm, Know you better by the end of it... how do I do that? Share a grand story? A life-altering epiphany? I dont know.... But then it got me thinking.... an icebreaker is ment for all of you to know about me... its so that we learn about each other and feel more connected. so ive decided this speech... it is not a speech....
Its an interview application so you all reach out to interact with me cause god knows im shy as hell and overthink everything way too much.

(Pause for laughter)

In all honesty though, I'm a simple guy. I don't like to hide behind fancy words or connotations. Im a guy with a mind who wants to work on projects and wishes to make a change in the world. There is a constant pull for me to understand things, to learn, and to build amazing ideas that help others with small problems.

And this fascination and interest on the world is reflected by the simple fact that i am interested in anything and everything... 

I mean Just the other day right here in YIH cafeteria, I bought a juice bottle. I then spent a solid 10 minutes admiring the cap, fascinated by the simple, ingenious flap designed to keep customers from hurting their fingers. 

That curiosity drives everything I love.

I love science, art, philosophy, and nature. People often separate science and art, but I follow Leonardo da Vinci’s philosophy: "Study the science of art, and the art of science." I’m no master artist, but I love putting pen strokes on paper when I'm bored.
Art in itslef though is diverse. Apart from the pen and paper I’m fascinated by rap music, the lyrical acrobatics and verbal tongue-gymnastics, its insane... And that same acrobatics and gymnastics when its moved and put to the body i love dance, because it takes a lot of physical strenght and agility and practice and the way people control their bodies with pure agility it mesmerizes me.
Funniliy enough i want to learn dance to then learn martial arts. Funny right. But actual fighting is a lot about footwork and quick agility and movement. So if u can move fast, you can punch fast and dodge faster. I know im spiraling with this speech but thats just the kind of person i am... Connect topics, take ideas, break them and find connections, join it back and learn new information. Thats what i do in life and in my mind
To me, your mind is a computer system: Garbage in, garbage out. If you feed your eyes ears and nose with craftsmanship, art, and meaning, that optimism is what you emanate back into the world. So good things in good things out... but also bad things in bad things out. That idea keeps you on your toes. Makes you really think what do u want to do and focus on those.

It brings me to another perspective in life and that is that I don’t believe in one global truth, only view points. And my main view point is simple: you are the product of your actions. It's a scary thought, but an empowering one. Because if u are a product of your actions... then u are in control. and ur actions every day matter a lot.

As you can all see. There is no purpose or meaningful story to this icebreaker speech. I just wanted to share who I am as a person. I stand here today with a healthy mind, a loving family back home, and a hungry brain. I might overthink, and I might be shy, but I want to grow, build lasting relationships, and make genuine friends here. 

Thank you, and I look forward to meeting you all!
