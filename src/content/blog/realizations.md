> A fool in motion beats a genius at rest.\
> Feelings always follow actions.\
> Screw your mood, follow the plan.

It's so easy to say, but insanely difficult in practice. Even something as simple as a 10 minute run every morning. But when the time actually arrives, doing it feels monumental. In the moment, we defer and say things like "oh nooo i had so much work" or "oh no my sleep is bad" or "oh no my time i missed it"...

Genuinely, it's frustrating. And the most frustrating thing is when you say to yourself _"We will do it"_ and then _break_ your word to **yourself**. Do that enough times and the simple thought becomes a habit, the habit becomes your attitude, and that attitude determines the path of your life... It's scary to think about.

<p class="section-break" aria-hidden="true">✦</p>

I came into NUS 3 months ago, on 26th July 2026...

And there are things I'm genuinely proud of. But also things that I'm deeply unhappy with... In many ways I took things too deep. I took words and ideas too personally... I lost myself in sadness, got plagued by distractions and drowned alone.

Heck, in the first weeks of October I went 4 days eating only 3 meals in total.
Foolish, in hindsight. I slept late, woke up later, there were days I locked myself in my 1 room with 5 walls and the blinds down, with only me, my thoughts and a 5 inch screen that can numb all your pain...
The phone is a disaster when you want to handle your sadness, but that is a talk for another journal.
I mean imagine waking up at 10am for a 9am exam... That happened.

But what use exists in crying about the past? And how can I, when younger me has dreams and older me has expectations? My semester 1... was a dumpster fire. I learnt many lessons... the VERY hard way, and met many exceptional people.

I learnt envy and jealousy. I learnt self-deprecation and disconnection. I learnt the fallacy in my own righteousness, and the ignorance behind my self-worth.

It's so easy to be blinded by another's blazing light, then look at your own star and feel less than what you are. But actually, it's the opposite. When you see another light burn brightly, you should chase it, to make yours just as bright. You should learn from others how to grow...

<p class="section-break" aria-hidden="true">✦</p>

There is a story in the Bible about Samson. A man chosen by God, does wrong, goes bad, then one day God leaves him, but he wakes up and tries again, believing God is still with him.

It's scary to think, but the truth is time waits for no one... People will run and rush, and if you stagnate you will be left behind. But at the same time, you cannot wish to go back... Instead there's today, this very moment.

**This is the youngest you will ever be.**

I don't want the ones I love to turn their backs on me. I don't wish for God to look away from me.

In one of the Rocky movies, I don't remember which, there's a famous line:

> There is no tomorrow.

Make plans, write ideas... but ultimately do the actions now, because THERE IS NO TOMORROW...

People die in this world. A mother wakes up with cancer, a child gets into an accident on the road. A father has a stroke... No one really knows when your body will give out, when you will stop seeing your loved ones, or when the world will take your life... Many people dream of being in the position you're in.

The question you have to ask yourself... if they were in your position, who would handle your life better? And if the answer is not you... then that is a sad realisation to make.

8 billion people in this world... and very few at the top. Very few survive the ruthless, cut-throat game. Only a few stand at the top. I hate thinking about why I'm not standing there... I don't want to stand down.

I don't want to bend or buckle to another's authority. I don't want to hide from my truth or ugliness. I want to be the flower that blooms no matter the ground. I want to be like the cactus that grows in the harsh desert sands, the banyan tree that stands at the centre, withstanding the test of time, sheltering the animals and birds that live in peace beneath it.

I want to grow. I want to learn. I want.......... NO.

<p class="section-break" aria-hidden="true">✦</p>

I WILL GROW, I AM GROWING.\
I WILL LEARN, AND I'VE STARTED LEARNING.\
I WANT, AND I HAVE. I AM BLESSED IN LOVE,\
AND I WILL FIND THE GOLD THAT AWAITS MY HEART.

I will speak what I want to speak, and I WON'T hide away.\
I will make myself so good that people have to accept me.\
I will make myself so good that I accept me.\
And for all the good and bad, I love the people around me.\
I love the blessings given to me. And I want to work to make the best\
of the love and kindness I've been given!
